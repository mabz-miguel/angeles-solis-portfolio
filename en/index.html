Profile

A practice shaped by a sensitivity to art and a commitment to its preservation over time.

Ángeles Solís Parra’s practice is best understood through more than three decades of professional experience: conserving and restoring sculpture, paintings and historic plaster casts, caring for institutional collections, and advancing research and the sharing of knowledge.

“Conservation means understanding a work in all its dimensions, so that every decision respects its history and safeguards its future.”

Research and Methodology

Knowledge grounded in conservation practice

The development of Anjusil®, alongside research and teaching, grows out of decades of professional practice dedicated to conserving and restoring historic plaster cast collections.

Methodology

Anjusil® — A cleaning methodology for historic plaster casts

Developed during the RABASF restoration project and subsequently used at the University of Seville and the MNRA. Associated with the National Award and Europa Nostra recognition.

Research

Material analysis and documentation of historic plaster casts

Development of diagnostic protocols and material testing methods for institutional collections in Spain and across Europe.

Teaching

Conservation education and institutional training

Contributing to the training of conservation professionals through academic institutions and heritage organisations.
